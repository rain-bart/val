My dearest Joanne Nicole S. Olores,

There are things in this world that are difficult to say out loud not because they aren't true, but because they are so deeply felt that words seem too small to carry them. This is my attempt, anyway.

I think about the way you smile when something genuinely makes you happy how it reaches your eyes before it even reaches your lips. I think about how much lighter everything feels when you're around. I think about how I never want to stop learning who you are.

You are not just someone I care about my Coleen. You are the reason I want to be better, kinder, more present. You are the person I look for in a crowded room. You are the first thought I have in the morning and the warmest one I carry through the day.

I know I'm just your CS boyfriend, more comfortable with code than with poetry but even I know that what I feel for you is something I never want to debug or rewrite. It's exactly right, just the way it is.

Even though we are far from each other, you are always close to my heart. The distance is hard sometimes, but loving you is easy every single day. I miss your smile, your voice, and the way you make me feel safe and happy. You are my favorite person, my best friend, and my biggest blessing.

Thank you for loving me, trusting me, and staying strong with me. One day, we won't have to say "I miss you" anymore. We will finally be together, and that day will be worth the wait.

So this Valentine's Day, I made you this little corner of the internet just for you, just from me because you deserve to feel as extraordinary as you truly are.

♥
Forever yours,
-Renny 🤍
